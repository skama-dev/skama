# Governance

This document describes how the Skama project is run and how decisions are made.

## Roles

**Contributors** are everyone who takes part in the project by opening issues, joining discussions, reviewing or submitting pull requests.

**Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, triage issues, cut releases and are responsible for the project's direction. They are members of the [@skama-dev/maintainers](https://github.com/orgs/skama-dev/teams/maintainers) team and are listed in [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS).

Skama currently has a single maintainer, its creator, [@javierblancodev](https://github.com/javierblancodev).

## Decision making

- Day-to-day decisions are made in the open, in issues and pull requests, by lazy consensus: a proposal goes ahead if no maintainer objects after a reasonable time for review.
- Significant decisions, such as changes to the architecture, the public API or the release process, are recorded as architecture decision records in `docs/adr/`, so that the context and the reasoning are kept.
- When consensus cannot be reached, the maintainers make the final decision.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained, high-quality contributions and have shown good judgement in reviews and discussions may be invited to become maintainers by the existing maintainers.

## Code of conduct

Everyone in the project follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Changes to this document

Changes to this governance model are proposed through a pull request and require the approval of the maintainers.
